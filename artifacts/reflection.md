# Global Energy Decision Atlas Reflection

The dataset supports a useful first-pass comparison of 15 national markets. It can show where reported electricity prices, electricity-generation sources, absolute energy demand, and net trade positions differ. Those comparisons help a business team form a shortlist and identify the questions that need local due diligence.

The data does not establish why one country has a higher retail price, whether a grid will be reliable for a particular facility, or how tariffs will change. The measures also come from different reference years. National consumption is not normalized for population or industry, and the residual “other” generation category combines nuclear power with other renewable sources. Iran’s retail prices are missing, so the dashboard leaves them out of price calculations instead of treating them as zero.

The most responsible use of the atlas is screening, not ranking. A team can compare cost, generation mix, and import exposure without collapsing them into an unsupported score. It should then validate industrial tariffs, demand charges, procurement options, and continuity requirements for the actual site.
